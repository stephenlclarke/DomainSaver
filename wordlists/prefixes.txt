# prefixes.txt - short words that go IN FRONT of a name you already want.
#
# When the name you actually want is taken (and it is), the cheapest rescue is
# a one-syllable prefix. "my", "get" and "try" are the three the startup world
# wore out, so they are also the three most likely to be registered already -
# they are near the top anyway because they are still the most natural to say.
# The further down this list you go, the better your odds.
#
# Used with generate.sh:
#   ./scripts/generate.sh --affix shed --prefixes wordlists/prefixes.txt --tlds com
#   ./scripts/generate.sh --affix shed --prefixes wordlists/prefixes.txt \
#       --suffixes wordlists/suffixes.txt --tlds dev --join -
#
# Format: one label per line, lowercase a-z 0-9 and hyphen. '#' starts a
# comment, to end of line. No dots - these are labels, not domains.
